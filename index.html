SCOPE OF WORK

I'm a mechanical engineering senior at UNC Charlotte who treats every problem like a drawing: define the geometry, state the loads, tolerance the result.

My work lives where design intent meets manufacturing reality — mechanism synthesis, FEA-driven iteration, and prototypes machined and assembled by hand. I care about drawings a machinist never has to call about, and analyses that hold up under test data.

Currently: finishing my capstone, seeking full-time mechanical design roles starting May 2027.
